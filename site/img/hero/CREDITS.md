The slideshow behind the hero of the home page (/, /fr/, /ar/) and of IRLY Girl (/girl, /fr/girl).
From IRLY's photo library (public-photos/v, sources in .github/scripts/media-variants-picks.json and

commercial use, no attribution required. Credited with thanks.

Each view: `<name>-1920.webp` and `<name>-960.webp` (16:10, landscape screens) and `<name>-p.webp`
(810 × 1440 crop for phones), made from the 2400 px originals.
