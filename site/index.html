Questions about IRLY

What is IRLY?

IRLY is an app to meet people who share your interests and do things together in real life: join communities, find or create activities, and talk with the group before you meet.

Is IRLY a dating app?

No. IRLY is about friendship, communities, activities and networking. People join to find friends, sport partners, people from their field or a group to explore the city with.

Where is IRLY available?

IRLY starts in Dubai and covers the other emirates of the UAE. It is also available in Bali.

Can I use IRLY now?

Yes. IRLY already works in your browser at getirly.com/app. The iOS and Android apps are coming soon.

How does IRLY keep members safe?

You choose who can see your profile and who can message you, and your exact location is never shared. You can report or block anyone, and reports are reviewed by the IRLY team.

What is IRLY Girl?

IRLY Girl is a space reserved for women: women-only communities, activities and conversations, to meet friends in a setting that feels safe.
