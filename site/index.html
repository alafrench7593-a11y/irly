Why IRLY

A city of millions.
Dinner for one.

Dubai is built for ambition: a new job, a new apartment, a new life, often far from family and old friends. The calendar fills up with work. The weekends stay empty. Making friends as an adult, in a city where almost everyone arrived from somewhere else, is harder than it looks.
