Why it is hard here

Everyone is busy.

Long days at work, long drives home. Weeknights vanish and weekends fill up fast.

People come and go.

A contract ends, a friend moves on. In a city of newcomers, you often start over.

Networking feels like a transaction.

Crowded events, business cards, small talk. Few conversations turn into people you see again.

Nobody talks to strangers.

Malls, gyms and cafés are full, yet everyone keeps to their own table. Meeting new people rarely just happens.

Making friends, finding a padel partner or meeting the right people for your work shouldn’t depend on luck.

04 How it works

Seven screens. One idea: go.

SCENE 1

What’s happening around you.

Open IRLY and see who is out right now, and what starts next near you.

SCENE 2

People near you.

Newcomers, locals, professionals. Their interests, their neighbourhood, what you have in common.

SCENE 3

Things to do, tonight.

Sport, food, culture, wellness. Every plan shows who is going and how many spots are left.

SCENE 4

Your city, live.

Plans, people and places on a real map of Dubai. Never an exact address.

SCENE 5

Create a plan in seconds.

Pick an activity, a time and a place. IRLY finds the people.

SCENE 6

Communities that meet.

Runners, founders, French speakers, gamers. People who actually see each other.

SCENE 7

A profile that says “let’s go”.

Who you are, what you love doing, what you’re looking for. No likes, no followers.

Why IRLY?

IRLY started with a simple observation. People want to meet new people. But the apps they use are built to keep them scrolling, not to get them out the door.

IRLY flips that. Every screen leads to a plan, a place and people you can actually meet.

Less scrolling.
