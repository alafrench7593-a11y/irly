**Description**:
IRLY helps you meet people in real life. Whether you just moved to a new city or want to grow your circle, IRLY brings together people who like the same things, around plans you can actually join.

COMMUNITIES
Join IRLY's communities in your city, from newcomers and expats to running, football, food, tech and culture, or start your own. Each community has its own chat, posts and plans.

ACTIVITIES
Find activities near you, see who is going and join in one tap. Create your own plan, a run, a padel game, a brunch, choose the place, the time and the number of spots, and a group chat opens for everyone going.

LIVE
See who is out right now near you: a photo or a few words, your neighbourhood only, gone after 4 hours. Join them or say hi.

MESSAGES
Private chats with friends, group chats and community chats, with photos.

PROFILE
Your photo, a short bio, your interests and the activities and communities you are part of. You choose who can see your profile and the activities you join.

IRLY GIRL
Spaces reserved for women: women-only communities, plans and a women-only Live.

SAFE BY DESIGN
Report or block any member or content in two taps. Your exact location is never shared: at most your neighbourhood. You can delete your account and your data from the app at any time.

IRLY is available in English and French, starting with Dubai and the other emirates of the UAE.

## 5. Compte de revue Apple

1. Choisis un mot de passe de 14 caractères ou plus, et ajoute-le dans GitHub : Settings → Secrets and variables → Actions → New repository secret → `REVIEW_ACCOUNT_PASSWORD`.
2. Lance GitHub → Actions → **Review account** → Run workflow. Le compte `appreview@getirly.com` est créé ou mis à jour, masqué des membres, avec sa bio « IRLY test account for the App Store review ».
3. Dans App Store Connect → ton app → onglet de la version → **App Review Information** : coche « Sign-in required », User name `appreview@getirly.com`, Password : le même mot de passe.

**Notes for App Review** (à coller tel quel) :

> IRLY helps people meet in real life through communities, group activities and friend connections, in Dubai and across the UAE.
>
> Sign in: open the app, choose the Emirates as destination (Bali is shown as coming soon), go through the short welcome, then the avatar at the top left (Profile) → "Sign in or create an account" → email and password with the demo account provided. The demo account is set in Dubai and is hidden from other members.
>
> What to test:
> - Home and Discover: categories, recommended plans and places.
> - Communities (from Home, top left): "For you" recommendations, "All" with search and themes, "Mine". Joining is always the user's choice.
> - Activities: open any activity, see who is going, join; create your own plan with the Create button on Home.
> - Friends: "Add friend" on any member profile sends a request the other member must accept.
> - Features marked "Coming soon" (Match IRLY on the centre button, IRLY Match inside IRLY Girl, Bali, and the IRLY PRO, Bon plan, Visa and Location services) are announced only: they show what is planned and are not part of this version.
> - Messages (top right): private, group and community chats.
> - Profile and Settings (gear): privacy controls, notification settings, blocked members, delete account (Settings → My account → Delete account).
>
> Safety: every profile, post, comment and message can be reported or its author blocked from the "…" menu or a long press; reports are reviewed by the IRLY team. Users must be 18 or older. Exact location is never shared.
>
> Permissions: location (centre the map, optional), camera and photos (profile photo, posts, profile verification selfie), notifications (asked after a first friend request, join or message, never at launch).
>
> Known limitations: content depends on real members in each city, so some lists may be short. IRLY Girl spaces are only available to members who registered as women.

**Étiquettes de confidentialité (App Privacy)** — données liées à l'utilisateur, utilisées pour le fonctionnement de l'app, non utilisées pour le suivi (tracking) :
Contact (e-mail uniquement : pas de connexion par téléphone) · Contenu utilisateur (photos, messages, publications) · Identifiants (ID utilisateur) · Localisation approximative (quartier choisi ; la position exacte n'est pas envoyée) · Données d'utilisation (interactions dans l'app) · Diagnostic si un outil de crash est ajouté plus tard.
À confirmer ligne à ligne avec la politique de confidentialité avant l'envoi.
