# Blog plan: loneliness and making friends in Dubai

The blog posts are short (400 to 600 words), in English and French, in `site/guides/blog.mjs`. The daily routine publishes two posts per day by taking the first topics below that are not yet ticked, then ticks them. When fewer than 6 remain, it adds 10 new distinct questions at the end.

## Rules for each post
- **One precise question per post** (a long-tail search). Never re-cover a topic that a guide or an earlier post already answers: that would compete with our own pages in Google.
- **Structure:**
  - an introduction that answers the question in 2 or 3 sentences;
  - 3 or 4 explicit H2s;
  - at least 2 internal links to the pillar guides (`dubai/lonely`, `dubai/make-friends`, `dubai/group-activities`, `dubai/going-out-alone`, and their French versions);
  - a CTA.
- **Content limits:**
  - no statistics, testimonials, named venues, prices or facts that were not checked;
  - general, verifiable advice only;
  - IRLY is presented as one option among others.
- **Paths:** `blog/<english-slug>/` and `fr/blog/<french-slug>/`, with the same `pair`.
